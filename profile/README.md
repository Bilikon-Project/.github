### 🎯 Our Mission

**Bilikon-Project** was founded by developers who believe that technology and knowledge should be accessible to everyone. We build practical, free, and open-source tools designed to work seamlessly both **online** and in **offline** environments where internet access is limited.

### 🛠️ What We Do

* **🌐 Online Projects:** We develop web applications, API services, and community-driven digital platforms.
* **📴 Offline Solutions:** We create standalone desktop tools, local network utilities, and offline-ready documentation packages.
* **🤝 Community & Support:** We help people solve real-world problems, mentor newcomers, and encourage contributions to the open-source ecosystem.

### 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome everyone who wants to build with us and help others:

1. **Explore Our Repositories:** Check out our projects and give a star (⭐) to support our work.
2. **Report Issues & Ideas:** Share bug reports or suggest new features through the **Issues** tab.
3. **Submit Pull Requests:** Fork our repositories, make improvements, and submit a **Pull Request**.
