# Bilikon <3 open source

Bilikon is an open-source community dedicated to sharing knowledge, building together, and making technology more accessible for everyone.
We create practical tools and clear guides rooted in real-world needs, without limiting ourselves to a single format or workflow.
And it is all powered by open source and shaped by people who care about helping others.

## Participating in the community

We want to build a warm, welcoming, and inclusive space where everyone feels comfortable. Being a beginner is completely fine—ask questions, share ideas, or jump in with small contributions whenever you feel ready.

## Get in touch!

Check out our projects and repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/Bilikon-Project/repositories).
If you spot a bug, have an idea to share, or want to build something together, our doors are always open via **Issues** and **Pull Requests**.

Building together, growing together.
